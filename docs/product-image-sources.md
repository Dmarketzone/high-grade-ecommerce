# Product image sources

Downloaded November 2, 2026 from official manufacturer product pages and their linked image hosts. The user specifically authorized authentic manufacturer photographs for the client-review storefront. This is not a representation that commercial publication rights have been granted. No placeholder product drawings, stock photography, AI-generated product images, or invented renders are used. Assets retain the exact model represented by the official gallery. The ONE 4|8 DSP record was replaced by ONE 6|10 DSP because the former official gallery reused files named for 6|10; the new model and source page agree.

Images were optimized from the official originals with aspect ratio preserved. Uniform white or transparent edge padding on 36 Focal and UNIDEN images was conservatively trimmed at threshold 2, with an additional 1% source-image safety border, then centered with approximately 7% margins on a 1100 × 900 canvas. The other 29 dark reflective or non-uniform photographs retain their complete original frame. No product hardware, text, logos, or design details were removed or retouched. Local gallery assets total 65 files and 5.54 MiB.

Every source below returned successful HTTP and decoded as an image through Sharp. All 65 gallery images were visually inspected on contact sheets after trimming, and their recorded dimensions match the actual files. The Mosconi One model mismatch was reviewed separately.
