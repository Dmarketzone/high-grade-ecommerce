  and XC does not appear among the modules on the

  at the time of review. That is insufficient to assert current dealer supply;
  its record explicitly requires lifecycle confirmation.

  and is not reproduced in this storefront.
- [DroneMobile subscriptions](https://www.dronemobile.com/subscriptions) explain
  that LTE connectivity needs a service/data plan. Control and security functions
  can require additional compatible hardware. No service plan is advertised as
  free or included, and no subscription pricing is copied into this demo.
- Other products are listed in the specified manufacturer families. A public
  product page does not prove an active distributor SKU, local stock, a dealer
  agreement, or regional availability.

## Fitment boundaries

Focal Inside is a separate OEM category. Utopia M and K2 products are custom
system components and must not inherit Inside's Plug & Play claims. Even Inside
records do not match a vehicle selection automatically. Exact year, model,
generation, body style, mounting position, factory audio package, connectors, and
required integration hardware need authoritative fitment data and an installer
review. Porsche, BMW, and Mercedes-Benz selector choices remain unverified
consultation inputs rather than confirmed product matches.
